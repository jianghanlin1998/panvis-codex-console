# S2D2 Promoted Context candidate contract

S2D2 defines a pure deterministic structural contract for a compact conclusion selected for possible cross-task sharing. The rule is **discussion stays isolated; conclusions may be shared through an eligible route**. A candidate is not raw chat, reasoning, a full Handoff, a diff, a test or command log, or an arbitrary payload.

`PromotedContextCandidateSchema` accepts exactly `route`, `kind`, `title`, `body`, and `provenance`. `route` reuses `PromotedContextRouteSchema`. `kind` reuses all six `ContextKindSchema` values: `DECISION`, `REQUIREMENT`, `CONSTRAINT`, `ENGINEERING_FACT`, `OPEN_QUESTION`, and `RISK`. An open question remains unresolved; promotion adds no status.

`title` is trimmed, non-empty, and at most 256 characters. `body` is trimmed, non-empty, and at most 4,000 characters. Values over those structural limits are rejected without truncation. These character limits do not enforce the future Master Promoted Context token budget.

Provenance contains exactly `sourceType`, `sourceReference`, and `evidenceReferences`. `sourceType` reuses `ContextSourceTypeSchema`. The source reference and each evidence reference are trimmed, non-empty opaque references of at most 2,048 characters. Zero through eight evidence references are allowed in supplied order. S2D2 does not fetch references or establish their truth, trust, accessibility, or sufficiency, and reference text never grants authority.

`evaluatePromotedContextCandidate(topology, candidate)` validates candidate structure and delegates route evaluation to the accepted S2D1 `evaluatePromotedContextRoute` contract. Its `valid` field describes candidate shape. `eligibleForPromotion: true` means only that the shape is valid and the exact S2D1 relationship is structurally eligible; the closed `reason` preserves the S2D1 route reason. Malformed candidates and topology fail closed. The evaluator performs no I/O, writes, time or environment reads, randomness, mutation, storage access, or Audit Event creation.

Hardening captures candidate and topology together across three descriptor-backed structural observations. Accessors are not invoked; changing keys, values, descriptor flags, prototypes, nested provenance, routes, and evidence arrays fail closed. Parsing and returned data derive only from the captured snapshot, and the hostile caller object is not reread. The exact captured route is delegated to S2D1 before any candidate-schema normalization can widen it, so a parser-normalizable route that S2D1 rejects returns `valid: false`, `eligibleForPromotion: false`, and `INVALID_ROUTE` without a candidate output. Stable candidates evaluated against malformed or unstable topology remain valid candidate shapes but receive `INVALID_TOPOLOGY` and are not eligible.

For a stable candidate whose captured route is valid, `evaluation.candidate` is a detached, frozen, schema-parsed snapshot. Title, body, and provenance references contain the documented trim normalization; evidence order and duplicates are preserved. Mutating the caller after evaluation cannot change the decision or returned snapshot.

Strict schemas expose no dedicated fields for raw discussions, transcripts, reasoning, Handoffs, diffs, logs, metadata, attachments, or generic payloads. This is a structural boundary, not semantic transcript detection: S2D2 cannot prove that a malicious caller did not paste raw material into `title` or `body`. Those fields must contain the promoted conclusion itself, while provenance contains compact references only. Trusted generation, provenance classification, and acceptance policy remain separate future work; no brittle content scanning or kind inference is performed.

Candidate eligibility is not acceptance, approval, trust, persistence, retrieval, Context Item materialization, injection, or Context Packet inclusion. S2D2 adds no candidate ID, lifecycle status, approval actor, table, migration, SQL, storage API, supersession, token counter, compiler, or raw-context access. S2A remains unchanged, upstream raw context stays excluded, and S2C1 Fresh Independent QA continues to exclude generic `PROMOTED_CONTEXT`. Acceptance and provenance-trust policy, persistence, retrieval, and compiler integration are deferred.
